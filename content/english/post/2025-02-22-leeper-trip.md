February 2025 brought a trip up to Leeper in northwestern Pennsylvania. It's the kind of place you go when you want to get away from everything - small town, surrounded by state forest land, and quiet in a way that cities never are.

## Winter in the Woods

There's something special about the Pennsylvania woods in winter. The snow muffles everything, the bare trees let you see deep into the forest, and the cold keeps you honest about how long you stay outside.

Leeper sits in the heart of the Allegheny National Forest region. It's not a destination so much as a place you pass through on the way to somewhere even more remote. That's part of the appeal.

## Cabin Time

The trip was about slowing down more than anything else. No agenda, no schedule - just time to read, cook, and take walks when the weather allowed. Sometimes the best trips are the ones where you don't do much at all.

The drive up through Clarion County is beautiful any time of year, but winter adds a starkness that I appreciate. Everything feels more elemental when the leaves are down and the snow is up.

A good reset before heading back to reality.
