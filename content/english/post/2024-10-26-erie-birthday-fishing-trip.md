For my birthday this year, I headed up to Erie for a fishing trip. Late October is one of the best times to fish Lake Erie - the crowds thin out, the fall colors are peaking, and the fish are active before winter sets in.

## Early Morning on the Water

We got out early to make the most of the day. The morning light on the water was spectacular, with that golden autumn quality you only get this time of year. The lake was calm, which made for comfortable fishing conditions.

<div style="text-align:center">

## The Catches

The fishing didn't disappoint. Erie in the fall means lake trout and steelhead are running during their migration to the lake, and we managed to land some good ones throughout the day.

<div style="text-align:center">
    <img src="{{<s3cdn>}}/photography/2024_erie_birthday/20241026_122021.jpg" style="max-width: 600px;" loading="lazy">
    <p><em>Midday success - the cooler was filling up nicely.</em></p>
</div>

There's something special about fishing on your birthday. No obligations, no schedule to keep - just you, the water, and whatever decides to bite. The combination of good company, beautiful scenery, and actually catching fish made it one of the better birthdays I've had.

## Autumn Colors

<div style="text-align:center">
    <img src="{{<s3cdn>}}/photography/2024_erie_birthday/20241026_122044.jpg" style="max-width: 600px;" loading="lazy">
    <p><em>The fall foliage along the shoreline was at its peak.</em></p>
</div>

By late afternoon, we called it a day with a respectable haul. The drive back featured some of the best fall colors I've seen - the trees along the lake were putting on their final show before winter.

If you're looking for a birthday tradition that doesn't involve cake and crowds, I highly recommend an October fishing trip. Just bring layers - the lake can get chilly once the sun starts going down.
