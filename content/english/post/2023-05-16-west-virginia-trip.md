In May 2023, I headed out to West Virginia for a few days of exploring. West Virginia doesn't get the attention it deserves - it's genuinely wild, beautiful country with some of the best scenery on the East Coast.

## Into the Mountains

The thing about West Virginia is that getting there is half the adventure. The roads twist through hollows and over ridges, and you're constantly rounding a bend to find another stunning view. It's not a place you rush through.

## Waterfalls and Trails

The state is full of waterfalls, and I managed to hit a few of them. Spring is the perfect time - the water flow is strong from snowmelt and rain, and everything is green and lush.

There's a rawness to West Virginia that you don't find in more developed areas. The forests feel old, the rivers run clear, and you can drive for miles without seeing another car.

## Almost Heaven

John Denver wasn't wrong. There's something about these mountains that gets under your skin. The trip was short but it left me wanting to come back and explore more. There's a lot of state to cover, and I barely scratched the surface.
