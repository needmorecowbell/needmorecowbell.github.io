For my birthday this year, I headed up to Erie for a fishing trip.

### Early Morning on the Water

We got out early to make the most of the day. The morning light on the water was spectacular, with that golden autumn quality you only get this time of year. Exceptionally windy, but we had a nice spot to stay warm throughout the weekend.
### The Catches

The fishing didn't disappoint. Erie in the fall means lake Steelhead are running during their migration to the lake, and we managed to land some good ones throughout the day.

### Autumn Colors

By late afternoon, we called it a day with a respectable haul. The drive back featured some of the best fall colors I've seen - the trees along the lake were putting on their final show before winter.
