## Overview

This project involved properly waterproofing and reflooring the front porch. The original porch flooring was showing wear and water was getting through, so a complete reflooring with proper moisture barriers was needed.

## Materials

- Owens Corning WeatherLock Granular ice & water barrier
- APOC #30 roofing felt
- Zip System flashing tape
- Deck screws and fasteners

## Build Process

The porch reflooring followed standard waterproofing practices:

1. **Preparation** - Removed old flooring and inspected the substructure for any damage
2. **Underlayment** - Applied ice and water barrier to create a primary waterproof layer
3. **Felt layer** - Added #30 roofing felt for secondary protection
4. **Flashing** - Used Zip System flashing tape to seal all seams and edges, particularly around the door threshold
5. **Flooring** - Installed new porch decking on top of the waterproofing system

The Zip System flashing tape is particularly important around penetrations and edges where water tends to infiltrate.
