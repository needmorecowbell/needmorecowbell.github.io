## Overview

A straightforward exhaust system upgrade for my 2014 Ford F-150. The factory exhaust was functional but uninspiring, so I decided to swap it out for an aftermarket system that would give the truck a deeper, more aggressive tone while maintaining streetability.

## The Swap

The new exhaust arrived well-packaged, though unpacking in the living room proved challenging when a certain furry assistant decided the packing materials were more interesting than the parts themselves.

{{< video path="projects/truck_exhaust_swap/exhaust_unboxing.mp4" poster="projects/truck_exhaust_swap/exhaust_unboxing.thumb.jpg" >}}

The installation was done with some help from friends with access to a lift, making the job significantly easier than trying to do it on jack stands. Having proper clearance underneath the truck made removing the old system and fitting the new components much more manageable.

## Results

The new exhaust provided exactly what I was looking for: a noticeable improvement in sound at idle and under acceleration, without being obnoxious on long highway drives. The deeper rumble suits the truck well and makes it feel more substantial on the road.
