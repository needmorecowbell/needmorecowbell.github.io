## Overview

A simple but entertaining project to add a PA (public address) loudspeaker system to my truck. The setup includes a compact amplifier box with volume controls and an aux input, connected to a horn speaker that can be mounted externally. The system allows playback of any audio source through the speaker, from phone alerts to custom soundboards.

## The Build

The PA system consists of two main components:

1. **Amplifier Unit** - A compact metal box housing the amplifier circuitry with volume and tone controls, a power LED indicator, and input jacks. The amp runs on 12V and can be powered directly from the truck's electrical system.

2. **Horn Speaker** - A weatherproof black horn/siren speaker designed for vehicle mounting. These are the same style used in emergency vehicles and can produce loud, clear audio that cuts through ambient noise.

The amplifier accepts standard 3.5mm aux input, making it easy to connect a phone or any other audio source. A braided aux cable provides the connection between the phone and the amplifier.

## Results

The finished system works great for its intended purpose - making announcements, playing sound effects, or just having fun with friends. The horn speaker is loud enough to be heard from a good distance, and the aux input makes it versatile for any audio source you want to broadcast.
