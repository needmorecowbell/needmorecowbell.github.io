## Content

This project involved transforming a beautiful mahogany wood slab into a functional computer desk. The natural live edges of the slab add character and warmth to the workspace.

### Materials

- Live-edge mahogany wood slab
- Steel hairpin legs (28")
- Wood finish/sealant
- Mounting hardware

### Build Process

The slab required sanding to smooth the surface while preserving the natural character of the wood grain and live edges. After achieving a smooth working surface, several coats of finish were applied to protect the wood and bring out its rich color.

The steel hairpin legs provide a modern industrial contrast to the organic wood slab, and the 28" height works well for a standard desk setup.
