## Content

This project was an experiment in customizing a classic Zippo lighter with natural wood veneer. The goal was to create a unique, handcrafted aesthetic while maintaining the full functionality of the lighter.

### Materials

- Standard Zippo lighter
- Maple wood veneer
- Superglue (CA glue)
- Sandpaper (various grits)
- Wood finish/sealant

### Build Process

The maple veneer was cut to size to fit the front and back panels of the Zippo casing. Before applying the wood, the metal surface was roughed up with sandpaper to create better adhesion for the superglue.

Each panel was carefully positioned and glued to the lighter body. After the adhesive cured, the edges were sanded flush with the metal casing and the wood was finished to protect it and bring out the natural grain pattern.

### Results

The finished lighter has a warm, natural look with the beautiful maple grain visible on both sides. The wood-to-metal transition at the edges is clean, and the lighter opens and closes smoothly without interference from the veneer.

### Lessons Learned

While the superglue adhesion worked initially and the lighter remained intact for several months of use, this method ultimately proved insufficient for long-term durability. Future attempts would benefit from using a stronger adhesive like epoxy, or potentially a different attachment method altogether.
