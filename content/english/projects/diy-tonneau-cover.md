## Overview

A quick and practical project to create a DIY soft tonneau cover for my 2014 Ford F-150. Rather than spending hundreds on a commercial tonneau cover, I built a simple but effective solution using a heavy-duty tarp and basic mounting hardware.

## The Build

The concept is straightforward: use a durable tarp material stretched taut across the truck bed rails. The dark material provides weather protection for cargo while maintaining a low-profile appearance.

Key features of this DIY approach:
- Heavy-duty tarp material for weather resistance
- Secured along the bed rails for a clean look
- Easy to install and remove when full bed access is needed
- Fraction of the cost of commercial tonneau covers

## Results

The DIY tonneau cover works well for basic cargo protection. While it doesn't have the locking security or sleek appearance of commercial hard or retractable covers, it gets the job done for keeping rain and debris off items in the bed. The total cost was minimal compared to even the cheapest aftermarket options.
